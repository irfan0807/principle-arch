# 06 - Reporting, Communication, and Career Growth

## Report Quality Standard
- Clear title and category
- Affected asset and scope confirmation
- Step-by-step reproduction
- Actual impact with business context
- Safe proof-of-concept evidence
- Remediation guidance

## What Makes Reports Get Accepted
- Deterministic repro steps
- Minimal assumptions
- No inflated severity claims
- Respectful and concise writing

## Remediation Guidance Style
- Immediate containment option
- Long-term engineering fix
- Verification strategy after patch

## Portfolio Building
- Keep sanitized writeups from labs/CTFs.
- Build a personal knowledge base.
- Track solved vulnerability patterns.
- Publish legal educational content.

## Career Paths
- Bug bounty specialist
- Pentester
- AppSec engineer
- Detection engineer
- Security researcher

## Interview Preparation
- Be ready to explain one real finding deeply.
- Practice threat modeling on sample systems.
- Explain secure-by-design improvements, not only vulnerabilities.
